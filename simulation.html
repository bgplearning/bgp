What it is

Simulations Built on the Science of Learning

Each simulation puts a learner face to face with a realistic AI character in a scenario built around your program's own learning objectives. Nothing is scripted for a single right answer. Learners ask questions, make judgment calls, and respond to what the character actually says, so every attempt plays out a little differently, just like a real conversation would.

That unpredictability is the point. Information is revealed gradually and only when a learner asks the right questions or reads the right cues, which mirrors how understanding actually builds in a real human interaction. Learners get to practice the hard parts of communication, like reading a room, following up on a vague answer, or knowing when to slow down, in a setting where getting it wrong the first time costs nothing.

Every simulation is designed in alignment with INACSL Standards of Best Practice and built to map directly onto your program's existing outcomes, so it drops into the coursework you already teach rather than asking you to build around it.
